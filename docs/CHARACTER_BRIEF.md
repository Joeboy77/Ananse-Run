# Character brief

Agreed with the owner on 2026-10-17 (ART_GUIDE Step A).

## Ananse

- **Age and build:** a wiry man in his 40s.
- **Face:** a sly half-grin and quick, clever eyes.
- **Skin:** warm dark brown.
- **Proportions:** slightly cartoonish, like Subway Surfers characters: larger head, hands and feet.
- **Clothing:** a kente-inspired cloth wrap in gold, green and red over one shoulder, simple sandals, and a small leather pouch of stories at his hip.
- **Spider touches (subtle):**
  - Four arms. The extra pair is tucked in and only visible when he swings (modelled as a separate object, `ananse_extra_arms.glb`).
  - A thin web pattern woven into the wrap.
  - Optionally, eight tiny bead "eyes" on a headband.

## Technical targets

- Under 15,000 triangles, one texture set at 1024 px or smaller (flat colours are fine and suit the style).
- About 1.8 units tall, standing at the origin, facing -Y in Blender.
- Two-armed main mesh for Mixamo rigging; extra arms as a separate file.
- Animations named exactly: `idle`, `run`, `jump`, `slide`, `strafe_left`, `strafe_right`, `stumble`, `crash`, `swing`, `victory`, `menu_idle`.
